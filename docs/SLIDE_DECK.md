## 5. Numéraire experiment — the headline finding (105 s) ⭐

**Figure:** the 3-bar eigenvalue-spectrum overlay + KPI strip from the
Numéraire Sensitivity panel on the Cross-Market page. Optional second
panel: the sector-purity per-numéraire bars (slide 7 detail).

**Numbers:**

| Numéraire | D_eff | Avg ρ | Top eig share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| TRY | 6.30 | 0.374 | **38.87%** |
| USD | 4.77 | 0.436 | **45.11%** |
| Gold | 3.71 | 0.502 | **51.47%** |

**Speaker note:** "The naïve prior is that re-expressing BIST returns
in a stable foreign numéraire — USD or gold — should *strip out* a
common factor and reduce the top eigenvalue's variance share. We took
the full BIST adjusted-close panel, divided every ticker's price by
USDTRY (and separately by gold-in-TRY), recomputed log-returns, and ran
the full 12-stage pipeline on each variant. The data says the
*opposite*: top eigenvalue share rises from 38.9% (TRY) to 45.1% (USD)
to 51.5% (gold), and the effective dimensionality drops from 6.3 to
3.7. The interpretation is finance-substantive: TRY volatility is a
**dispersion source** for BIST equities — exporters benefit when the
Lira weakens, importers and rate-sensitive financials suffer, so the
currency leg creates *cross-sectional spread* rather than common
movement. Strip the currency and you uncover a single dominant global-
equity-risk factor that TRY noise was masking. This is the kind of
empirical question multi-numéraire portfolios actually face. We tested
it; the prior failed."

## 11. Engineering rigour — what we fixed (60 s)

**Three bullets:**

1. **Clustering pathology** — switched single-linkage chaining to
   Ward+20. BIST max cluster 45→12; S&P max cluster 446→36; ARI 8×
   higher (BIST) and 44× higher (S&P). The proposal-promised "banks
   cluster together" sanity check now passes.
2. **TE methodological hardening** — replaced `np.random.permutation`
   surrogate (destroyed source autocorrelation) with a circular-block-
   bootstrap (Politis & Romano 1992, block length 5). Added
   Benjamini–Hochberg FDR correction across the N×(N−1) directed pairs.
   Both fixes are config-driven via `transfer_entropy.surrogate_block_length`
   and `transfer_entropy.multiple_testing`.
3. **Numéraire experiment infrastructure** — `src/basis_transform.py`
   exposes a single `apply_numeraire` function with a mathematical
   identity test (the chain rule holds: `log(P/B).diff() ≡
   log(P).diff() − log(B).diff()`). Variants register as first-class
   universes in `app/universe_registry.py` with their own dashboard
   path.

**Reproducibility:**
- **196 tests passing**, single-command runs
  (`uv run python run_pipeline.py`)
- **5 universes** registered and rendered (BIST, S&P, BIST/USD,
  BIST/Gold, EEG)
- **Reproducible** end-to-end from raw data

**Three contributions:**

1. **Empirical:** the numéraire experiment — TRY-stripping *increases*

## Q&A backup slides (only if asked)

- A1: Why Ward + n_clusters=20? (Tested against the proposal's "banks
  cluster together" sanity check; Ward with maxclust criterion gives
  balanced cluster sizes and recovers sector structure cleanly.
  Fallback `complete` linkage tested as backup.)
- A2: Why BH-FDR not Bonferroni? (BH controls the *expected proportion*
  of false discoveries among rejections at level α rather than the
  family-wise error rate; appropriate when many of the hypotheses are
  expected to be true.)
- A3: Why XAU/TRY for the gold numéraire, not XAU/USD? (We compare
  BIST equity returns expressed in three units measuring local
  purchasing power: TRY, USD, and a hard asset. XAU/USD would conflate
  the FX swap with the gold leg.)
- A4: Why is D_eff invariant across BIST and S&P? (Both are
  diversified large-cap indices with ~3-4 dominant cross-sector modes
  plus a few isolated industries. The invariance suggests `D_eff ≈ 6`
  is a structural property of "modern diversified equity index" rather
  than a property of either specific market.)
- A5: What runs on Loihi 2 in practice? (Currently nothing — Loihi/
  TrueNorth/SpiNNaker are the target deployment substrates. The
  architecture is already neuromorphic-compatible: Σ-Δ-style spike
  encoders, LIF dynamics, recurrent topology with surrogate-gradient
  training. The readout layer would need rate-to-spike conversion
  before physical deployment.)
- A6: Why the regime KL ratio Türkiye-quake / Ukraine = 1.85? (The
  Türkiye earthquake was a domestic shock that disproportionately hit
  rate-sensitive financials, construction, and insurance — sectors
  with high BIST weight. Ukraine was an external shock transmitted via
  commodity channels, which hit fewer BIST sectors directly. KL
  quantifies the joint-distribution shift, not just the average
  correlation move.)
